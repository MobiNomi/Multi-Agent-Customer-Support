# Upgrading, downgrading and cancelling

Only the account owner can change or cancel the plan. All plan changes are made in Settings > Billing > Plan.

## Upgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately. You pay a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing cycle, and the new price applies from the next cycle.

Example: you are on Free and upgrade to Pro monthly halfway through the month. You are charged about half of $15 per user for the remaining days, then the full price from the next billing date.

To upgrade to Enterprise, ask the chat assistant to open a ticket or contact sales. Enterprise upgrades need a short onboarding call, so they are activated within 2 business days.

## Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. You keep your current features until then. There is no refund for the remaining time on the higher plan (see the refund policy for the 14-day annual exception).

When moving to Free, anything above the Free plan limits becomes read-only: for example, if you have 10 projects, 3 stay editable and 7 become read-only. Nothing is deleted.

## Switching between monthly and annual

- Monthly to annual: takes effect immediately. The unused part of the current month is credited against the annual price.
- Annual to monthly: takes effect at the end of the paid year.

## Cancelling your subscription

1. Go to Settings > Billing > Plan and choose "Cancel plan".
2. Pick a reason (optional) and confirm.
3. You receive a confirmation email.

After cancelling, you keep paid features until the end of the period you already paid for, and you are not charged again. The workspace then moves to the Free plan.

## What happens to your data after cancelling

Your data stays in your workspace on the Free plan. If you delete the workspace instead, the deletion can be undone for 7 days; after that the data is removed and erased from backups within 60 days. You can export everything at any time from Settings > Data > Export.

## Reactivating

You can resubscribe at any time from Settings > Billing > Plan. Read-only projects become editable again as soon as the payment succeeds.
