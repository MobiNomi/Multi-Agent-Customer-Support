# Plans and pricing

All prices are in US dollars (USD) and exclude any local taxes.

## Free plan

Price: $0, forever.

- Up to 3 projects and 2 team members.
- 1 GB of file storage.
- 100 automation runs per month.
- API access: 1,000 requests per day, burst limit 2 requests per second.
- Support: help centre, community forum and email (first response within 3 business days).

The Free plan is meant for individuals and very small teams trying AcmeCloud.

## Pro plan

Price: $15 per user per month billed monthly, or $149 per user per year billed annually (about 17% cheaper than monthly).

- Unlimited projects, up to 50 team members.
- 100 GB of file storage.
- 5,000 automation runs per month.
- API access: 50,000 requests per day, burst limit 10 requests per second.
- Guest access for clients, time tracking and custom fields.
- Support: email and chat (first response within 1 business day).

## Enterprise plan

Price: custom, starting at $2,500 per year. Contact sales through the website or ask the chat assistant to open a ticket.

- Unlimited projects, members and automation runs.
- 1 TB of storage (more on request).
- API access: 500,000 requests per day, burst limit 50 requests per second.
- Single sign-on (SAML SSO), audit logs and advanced permissions.
- Choice of data region (EU or US).
- 99.9% uptime service level agreement (SLA).
- Dedicated account manager and 24/7 phone support for Severity 1 incidents.
- Payment by card or bank transfer against invoice (net 30 days).

- Non-profits and registered educational institutions get 30% off Pro. Ask support for the application form.
