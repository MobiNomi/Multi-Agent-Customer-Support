# Billing and payments

## Accepted payment methods

- Credit and debit cards: Visa and Mastercard.
- PayPal.
- Bank transfer against invoice: Enterprise plan only, payment due within 30 days (net 30).

AcmeCloud charges in US dollars. If your card is in another currency, your bank converts the amount and may add a foreign transaction fee. AcmeCloud does not control or refund bank fees.

## Billing cycle

You are billed on the same day each month (monthly plans) or each year (annual plans), starting from the day you subscribed. For example, if you upgraded to Pro on 12 March, you are billed on the 12th of every month.

Adding team members in the middle of a cycle creates a prorated charge for the rest of that cycle.

## Invoices and receipts

An invoice is emailed to the account owner after every successful payment. You can also download all invoices as PDF from Settings > Billing > Invoices.

To add a company name, address or tax number to future invoices, go to Settings > Billing > Billing details. Invoices that were already issued cannot be edited, but support can reissue the most recent invoice once.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, AcmeCloud retries the card 3 times over 7 days and emails the account owner after each attempt. Common causes are an expired card, insufficient funds or the bank blocking an international payment.

If all retries fail, the workspace is moved to the Free plan. No data is deleted: projects above the Free plan limits become read-only until you update your payment method and resubscribe. Update your card in Settings > Billing > Payment method.

## Duplicate charges

A duplicate charge means the same order was charged twice. It usually happens when a payment times out and is retried, so the first attempt and the retry both go through.

How duplicates are handled:

1. The second payment is marked as "Duplicate" in the payment records.
2. The customer or the chat assistant opens a Billing ticket for a refund. The ticket should mention the order ID and the transaction ID of the duplicate payment.
3. The billing team confirms the duplicate, usually within 1 business day, and refunds it in full to the original payment method.
4. The refund appears on your statement within 5 to 7 business days after it is issued. Some banks take up to 10 business days.

Duplicate charges are always refunded in full. You do not need to cancel your plan.

## Pending charges

Some banks show a temporary "pending" authorisation when you add a card or retry a payment. Pending authorisations that are not completed disappear on their own, usually within 3 to 5 business days, and are not real charges.

## Taxes

Prices exclude taxes. Where AcmeCloud is required to collect sales tax or VAT, it is added at checkout and shown on the invoice. Enterprise customers can provide a tax exemption certificate to support.
