# Refund policy

This policy explains when AcmeCloud refunds a payment and how long refunds take.

## Annual plans

Annual Pro subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the purchase date. After 14 days, annual payments are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time to stop the plan renewing. You keep Pro features until the end of the paid year.

## Monthly plans

Monthly payments are not refunded once the billing month has started. If you cancel a monthly plan, you keep access until the end of the month you paid for and you are not charged again.

## Duplicate charges and billing errors

Duplicate charges and billing errors made by AcmeCloud are always refunded in full, at any time, regardless of plan. Examples: being charged twice for the same order, being charged after cancelling, or being charged the wrong amount.

## Enterprise contracts

Refunds for Enterprise plans follow the terms of the signed contract. Contact your account manager or ask the chat assistant to open a Billing ticket.

## Add-ons and one-time purchases

- Monthly add-ons (extra storage, extra automation runs) are not refunded for the current month, but can be removed at any time to stop future charges.
- Onboarding sessions can be refunded in full if cancelled at least 48 hours before the session. Later cancellations are not refunded but can be rescheduled once.

## What is not refunded

- Partial months or unused time after the 14-day window on annual plans.
- Bank fees, currency conversion fees and foreign transaction fees charged by your bank.
- Accounts closed for breaking the terms of service.

## How to request a refund

1. Ask the chat assistant, or email support@acmecloud.example, with your order ID (it starts with ORD-) and the reason.
2. A Billing ticket is opened. The chat assistant cannot issue refunds itself; it opens the ticket and gives you the ticket ID.
3. The billing team reviews the request, usually within 1 business day, and replies on the ticket.

## Refund timelines

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. AcmeCloud issues the refund within 1 business day of approval, and it appears on your statement within 5 to 7 business days. Some banks take up to 10 business days. Refunds to PayPal usually appear within 2 business days.

AcmeCloud cannot refund to a different card or account than the one used for the payment.
