# Account and security

## Resetting your password

1. On the login page, click "Forgot password".
2. Enter the email address of your account.
3. Open the reset link in the email. The link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once.

If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder and make sure you used the email address on your account. Support agents can never see or set your password.

## Account lockout

After 5 failed login attempts in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password. If you did not make those attempts, reset your password and turn on two-factor authentication.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

AcmeCloud supports 2FA with an authenticator app (for example Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator). Turn it on in Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication.

When you enable 2FA you get 10 one-time backup codes. Keep them safe: they are the only way to log in if you lose your phone. If you lose both your phone and your backup codes, the account owner must contact support and verify their identity, which takes up to 2 business days.

Workspace admins on Pro and Enterprise can require 2FA for all members.

## Single sign-on (SSO)

SAML single sign-on is available on the Enterprise plan only. It works with identity providers such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace. Your account manager helps set it up during onboarding.

## Changing your email address

Go to Settings > Profile > Email. AcmeCloud sends a confirmation link to the new address and a notice to the old address. The change takes effect when you click the link.

## Roles in a workspace

- Owner: one per workspace. Manages billing, plan changes and can delete the workspace.
- Admin: manages members, permissions and integrations.
- Member: works on projects they have access to.
- Guest (Pro and Enterprise): limited access to specific projects, for clients and freelancers.

To transfer ownership, the current owner goes to Settings > Members, opens the new owner's profile and chooses "Make owner".

## Deleting your account

Only the owner can delete a workspace, from Settings > Workspace > Delete workspace. Deletion has a 7-day grace period during which it can be undone. After that, data is kept for up to 60 days in backups and then permanently erased. Cancel any paid plan first to avoid further charges.

## Reporting a security issue

If you think your account was accessed by someone else, reset your password, sign out of all sessions from Settings > Security > Active sessions, and ask the chat assistant for a human agent so the security team can review it.
