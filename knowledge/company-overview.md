# Company overview and support hours

AcmeCloud is a project management and workflow automation platform for small and medium-sized teams. Teams use it to plan projects, assign tasks, automate repetitive work and connect other tools through the AcmeCloud REST API. AcmeCloud is a fictional company used for this demo.

## What AcmeCloud offers

- Projects, task boards, timelines and shared files.
- Automations that run actions when something happens, for example "when a task is marked done, notify the client".
- A REST API and webhooks for developers.
- Web app plus mobile apps for iOS and Android.

There are three plans: Free, Pro and Enterprise. See "Plans and pricing" for features and prices.

## How to contact support

- Chat assistant: available 24/7 on the website and in the app. It can check orders, payments and tickets and open tickets for the support team.
- Email: support@acmecloud.example
- Help centre: help.acmecloud.example
- System status: status.acmecloud.example
- Enterprise customers also have a dedicated account manager and a 24/7 phone line for critical incidents.

## Human support hours

Human agents work Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 Pakistan Standard Time (UTC+5). Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next working day. Public holidays in Pakistan are treated as non-working days.

Enterprise customers get 24/7 coverage for Severity 1 incidents (the service is down or data is at risk).

## Response times by plan

| Plan | Channels | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Help centre, community forum, email | Within 3 business days |
| Pro | Email and chat | Within 1 business day |
| Enterprise | Email, chat, phone, account manager | Within 4 business hours; Severity 1 within 1 hour, 24/7 |

Response targets are measured during human support hours unless stated otherwise.

## Ticket statuses

- Open: the ticket has been received and is waiting for an agent.
- Escalated: a human support specialist has been asked to take over. Escalated tickets are handled first.
- Closed: the issue is resolved. Replying to a closed ticket within 7 days reopens it.

Every ticket has an ID that starts with TCK-, for example TCK-9001. Quote the ticket ID when you contact support so the agent can find your case quickly.
