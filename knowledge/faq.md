# Frequently asked questions

## Is there a free trial of Pro?

Yes. New workspaces can try Pro free for 14 days without a card. At the end of the trial the workspace moves to the Free plan unless you subscribe.

## Can I pay in Pakistani rupees?

No. AcmeCloud charges in US dollars only. Your bank converts the amount and may add a foreign transaction fee.

## Do you offer discounts for students or non-profits?

Registered non-profits and educational institutions get 30% off the Pro plan. Individual student discounts are not available. Ask support for the application form.

## How do I add team members?

Owners and admins go to Settings > Members > Invite and enter email addresses. On Pro, each new member is added to your bill with a prorated charge for the current cycle.

## Is there a mobile app?

Yes, for iOS 16 or later and Android 10 or later. Search for "AcmeCloud" in the App Store or Google Play.

## Can I use AcmeCloud offline?

The mobile apps can show recently opened projects offline and sync changes when you reconnect. The web app needs an internet connection.

## What integrations are available?

Built-in integrations include Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Gmail and Zapier. Anything else can be connected through the REST API or webhooks.

## How do I get an invoice with my company details?

Add your company name, address and tax number in Settings > Billing > Billing details before the next payment. Future invoices will include them.

## Why was I charged twice?

Usually because a payment timed out and was retried, so both attempts went through. The duplicate is refunded in full. Ask the chat assistant to check your order and open a refund ticket.

## How long does a refund take?

Refunds are issued within 1 business day of approval and appear on your statement within 5 to 7 business days (up to 10 for some banks). PayPal refunds usually appear within 2 business days.

## Can I talk to a human?

Yes. Ask the chat assistant for a human agent and it will open an escalated ticket. Human agents work Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 Pakistan Standard Time.

## Where can I check if AcmeCloud is down?

At status.acmecloud.example. You can subscribe there to email updates about incidents and maintenance.
