# Privacy and data handling

## What data AcmeCloud stores

- Account data: name, email address, workspace name and billing details.
- Workspace content: projects, tasks, comments and files you upload.
- Usage data: log-in times, device type and feature usage, used to keep the service secure and improve it.

Card numbers are handled by the payment provider. AcmeCloud never stores full card numbers; only the last four digits and expiry date are shown in Settings > Billing.

## How data is protected

- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or newer) and at rest (AES-256).
- Backups are taken every day and kept for 30 days.
- Staff access to customer data is limited and logged. Support agents only open workspace content with your permission while working on a ticket.

## Where data is stored

Free and Pro workspaces are stored in the US region. Enterprise customers can choose the EU or US region during onboarding. Moving an existing workspace to another region is possible for Enterprise and takes up to 5 business days.

## Exporting your data

The owner or an admin can export all workspace data at any time from Settings > Data > Export. Exports are available as JSON (complete) or CSV (tasks and projects only). Large exports are prepared in the background and a download link is emailed; the link is valid for 7 days.

## Deleting your data

- Deleting a workspace starts a 7-day grace period. After that, data is removed from live systems and erased from backups within 60 days.
- Individual users can ask for their personal data to be deleted by emailing support@acmecloud.example. Requests are completed within 30 days.

## Sharing with third parties

AcmeCloud does not sell customer data. Data is shared only with service providers needed to run the service (hosting, payments, email delivery), under contracts that require them to protect it.

## Cookies

The web app uses essential cookies to keep you logged in. Analytics cookies are optional and can be turned off in Settings > Privacy.
